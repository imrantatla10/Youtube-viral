## 3. What works on these channels

These numbers cover all four channels together: 1,307 videos, with an overall median of 68.7K views.

| Pattern | Median views with it | Median views without it | Share of the top 50 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Animal in the title** | **91K** | 64K | **23 of 50** |
| **Title starts like a story** ("He…", "She…", "When…", "This…") | **114K** | 63K | 14 of 50 |
| **Emotional words** ("final", "last", "loyal", "never") | **103K** | 68K | 5 of 50 |
| Number in the title | 54K ⬇️ | 73K | 4 of 50 |
| Plain soldier/military title | 41K ⬇️ | 72K | 4 of 50 |

Titles in the top 50 are short: the median is 6 words.

🔎 **What this means for you**

1. **Emotion beats facts on these channels.** This is the opposite of Historic Combat. There, numbers and facts worked. Here, feelings work: loyalty, love, saying goodbye, cute animals, and funny moments.

2. **The biggest hits mix two topics.** Some examples:
   - Military + dog = 99.7M
   - War pilot + kindness = 28.8M
   - Army + golden retriever = 23.3M

   A plain military title only gets 41K. **Military history works best when you add an animal or a human heart to it.**

3. **Title style that works:** 3 to 7 words. Write it like the start of a story. Do not give away the ending.
   - "The Moose Ignored The Fence"
   - "Toby's Final Look"

4. **The formats that win most often:**
   - An animal does something surprising
   - A loyal animal or a sad goodbye
   - A funny moment with police or in court
   - A strange fact about the body or about a baby
   - A small hero with a big result

## 4. ⚠️ Important warnings: please read

1. **Never re-upload their clips.** Do not use their footage, and do not take a clip and add your own voice to it.
   - This gets you copyright strikes.
   - It also breaks YouTube's "reused content" rule, so the channel can never be monetized.
   - Use the **same topic**, but with your **own footage** (free stock or public domain) and your **own new script**.
2. **Do not copy their script and change a few words.** Facts cannot be copyrighted, but someone's writing can. Read the real story, then write it fresh. Every script below is written from scratch.
3. **Some viral stories may not be true.** For example, the "Biscuit, too happy for the Army" story (23.3M) gives a date and place, but I could not verify it.
   - Copying a made-up story can get your videos flagged.
   - It also makes your audience trust you less.
   - Every idea below is based on a **well-documented real event or scientific fact**.
